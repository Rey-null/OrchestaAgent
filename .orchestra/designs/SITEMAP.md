## Conventions

* Status values: `Draft`, `In Review`, `Proposed`, `Approved`, `Superseded`.
* A design is `Approved` only after a human approves it and no unsettled
  Blocker or Major finding remains.
* Record every review in a sibling `*_goldfish_review.md` file so the
  verification history stays next to the design.
